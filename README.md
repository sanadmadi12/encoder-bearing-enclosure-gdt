# Encoder & Bearing Enclosure

**Mechanical Design | GD&T | Additive Manufacturing**

## Overview

This project involved designing, fabricating, and validating a compact mechanical enclosure for an **AS5600 magnetic rotary encoder** and a **65 × 50 × 7 mm bearing**. The design prioritizes component alignment, secure mounting, repeatable assembly, and manufacturability using 3D printing.

## Engineering Objective

Develop an enclosure that:

- Accommodates the specified bearing and AS5600 encoder within a compact assembly.
- Maintains the alignment required for encoder integration.
- Provides fastening features for assembly using **M2 screws**.
- Incorporates dimensional and geometric tolerances appropriate for component fit and assembly.
- Can be fabricated and physically checked using additive manufacturing.

## Design and Engineering Approach

### CAD and Component Integration

The enclosure was modeled in **SolidWorks**, with dedicated features to locate and secure the bearing and encoder. The design considered assembly access, component positioning, and the relationship between the bearing support and encoder mounting geometry.

### Geometric Dimensioning and Tolerancing

**GD&T** principles were applied to define and evaluate critical geometric relationships affecting fit and alignment. Mechanical fit and tolerance analysis informed the enclosure geometry and its suitability for repeatable assembly.

### Fabrication and Validation

The enclosure was **3D printed** and assessed through physical assembly and fit checks. Validation focused on dimensional accuracy, encoder alignment, and the successful integration of the bearing and fasteners.

## Results

- Produced a functional 3D-printed enclosure integrating the bearing and AS5600 encoder.
- Confirmed dimensional fit and encoder alignment through physical validation.
- Successfully assembled the enclosure using the intended fastening approach.
- Demonstrated a design workflow connecting CAD modeling, tolerance considerations, additive manufacturing, and hands-on verification.
